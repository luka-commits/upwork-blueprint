# Google Ads delivery knowledge

Use this when an Upwork job includes Google Ads, paid search, PPC, campaign setup or account optimization. This is an implementation map, not a performance promise.

## What a credible plan must establish

1. **Business goal and economics:** conversion action, lead or sale value, service area, margin, sales capacity, monthly media budget and approval boundaries.
2. **Measurement first:** audit or install the Google tag and conversion actions, remove duplicate counting, define primary versus secondary conversions, and test each action before bids optimize toward it.
3. **Account structure:** group campaigns by business objective, budget or geography. Group ads and keywords by one coherent landing-page and creative theme. Avoid fragmentation that splits useful data.
4. **Demand and targeting:** map high-intent searches, locations, schedules, audiences and exclusions. Choose match types deliberately. Broad match depends on strong conversion data and bidding signals, not optimism.
5. **Landing path and creative:** align query, ad promise and landing page. Build responsive search ads and useful assets around the same offer and proof.
6. **Budget and bidding:** choose a launch strategy that fits the amount and quality of conversion data. Document daily budget, guardrails and the condition for changing strategy.
7. **Quality assurance and launch:** check policies, URLs, tracking, geography, networks, schedules, negatives and billing. Launch only after the measured conversion path works.
8. **Learning loop:** review search terms, add negative keywords, promote useful terms, inspect conversion quality and auction context, then test one meaningful hypothesis at a time.

## Rules for a pitch diagram

- Put conversion tracking before automated bidding.
- Show the line from search term to keyword/theme, ad, landing page, conversion and CRM outcome.
- Keep campaign objectives and ad-group themes visible without drawing every keyword.
- Give search-term review and negative keywords a recurring feedback loop.
- Mark the media budget as client spend, separate from implementation fees.
- Treat target CPA, ROAS, lead volume and cost forecasts as unknown until the account and conversion data support them.
