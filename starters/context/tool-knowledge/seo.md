# SEO delivery knowledge

Use this when an Upwork job includes SEO, local SEO, a website audit, keyword research, content architecture or organic growth. It is a compact implementation map distilled from the Jono SEO Blueprint. It belongs to this repository so the Upwork OS works on another member's machine without Jono installed.

## What a credible plan must establish

1. **Business context:** offer, buyer, service area, exclusions, website, competitors and usable proof. A gap stays a question.
2. **Access and baseline:** Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile when local, CMS, sitemap and robots. Freeze the measured starting point before changes.
3. **Site reality:** inventory the real URLs, index state, templates, technical health, internal links and conversion tracking. State whether a check is measured, sampled or inferred.
4. **Search demand:** use Search Console first for existing visibility and a keyword tool second for opportunity. Verify important intent on the live results page. Tool volumes rank opportunities; they do not predict visits.
5. **Build map:** one intent per page, money pages for services, local pages only where real local detail exists, and supporting content that links into the commercial pages. Check plausible page collisions before adding another URL.
6. **Page improvement:** answer the query clearly, add verified proof, tighten titles and metadata, make crawlable content available in the HTML, and connect the page into the internal-link graph.
7. **Publish and verify:** deploy, verify rendered HTML, sitemap, canonical, robots and indexability, then request indexing where appropriate.
8. **Learning loop:** compare Search Console, analytics and local results against the frozen baseline. Prioritize business outcomes and qualified enquiries over cosmetic scores.

## Job-specific lanes

- **Audit:** inventory, baseline, competitor benchmark, technical and on-page findings, impact order, then an approval gate before fixes.
- **Keyword strategy:** market and service confirmation, wide pull, live intent checks, realistic difficulty, clustering, cannibalization gate and a prioritized page map.
- **Local SEO:** canonical name/address/phone, categories, service area, map-pack competitors, reviews, citations, local landing pages and measurement.
- **Implementation:** map the approved pages and fixes to CMS changes, publishing, indexing, internal links and reporting.

## Rules for a pitch diagram

- Start with the client's assets and access, not with an SEO tool logo.
- Show a measured baseline before recommendations.
- Give live search intent and existing performance their own decision points.
- Separate audit, build, publish and learning-loop milestones.
- Mark missing access, business choices and unsupported claims as `to confirm` nodes.
- Never present keyword volume, difficulty, traffic estimates or AI visibility as guaranteed demand or results.
- For local work, show Google Business Profile and review operations as their own branch. They are not an on-page footnote.

## Evidence order

Use the live public site for current public commitments. An explicit owner correction can supersede it when the owner says the site is stale. Search Console and analytics outrank third-party traffic estimates. Preserve any conflict instead of averaging it away.
